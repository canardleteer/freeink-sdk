# M5Stack PaperColor

ESP32-S3. 400×600 Spectra-6 ED2208. Native driver interrupts the OTP
waveform at ~340 ms for reading-speed monochrome; a complete waveform is
~15 s and is required for DC balance / true color. Optional
`-DFREEINK_M5_OFFICIAL=1` swaps in M5GFX (`m5paper_official`); that is a
backend, not a second `DisplayController`. ES8311 + AW8737A audio, two RGB
LEDs. No touch, no frontlight.

Sample envs `m5paper` (native) and `m5paper_official`. Silicon has PSRAM;
the sample does not set `BOARD_HAS_PSRAM`. Framebuffer stays in DRAM.
